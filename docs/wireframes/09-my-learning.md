## States and checks

Missing recent lesson: offer next available lesson and course. Corrupt local records: preserve recoverable data, offer export/restore, and keep course access open. Storage unavailable: Temporary session banner with Download backup if current data can be exported. Do not claim sync or show a sign-in CTA.

Acceptance: returning learners can resume in one action and never have to complete a form to access a lesson.
