# Lesson-by-lesson diagram backlog — 6 October 2026

Companion to [the audit](diagram-audit-2026-10-06.md) and [detailed specifications](diagram-specifications-2026-10-06.md). All recommendations are proposed, not implemented. Every canonical lesson appears exactly once.

**P1:** materially improves a central mechanism or prevents a likely misunderstanding. **P2:** useful refinement or lower-priority extension. P1 is a teaching priority, not a claim of a numerical defect. Implement in the batches listed in the audit, not all at once.

**Keep:** retain the lead visual; annotations may help. **Refine:** improve the existing explanation/diagram. **Augment:** preserve the evidence plot and add a mechanism view. **Replace lead:** put a more appropriate representation first; retain useful measurements as supporting evidence.

Acceptance checks below apply to the proposed diagrams. They are not claimed to have been executed in this audit.
