Specializations.

## Lesson sequence

### 14.01 Read your first jaxpr

Status: planned brief.

**Learn and build:** Inspect a small transformed function and identify its operations.

**Evidence:** A reproducible experiment for “Read your first jaxpr”: code, environment, observed output, and an explanation of one deliberate change.

**Checkpoint:** Explain your result for “Read your first jaxpr”, identify one failure case, and show how you verified the fix.
